About the Catalog

Why these R&B beats?

The R&B catalog lives at the intersection of R&B and neo-soul — smooth enough to let a vocalist sit on top without fighting the mix, warm enough to carry a whole record on feel alone. Expect live-feeling drums, rich chord work on keys and Rhodes, tasteful guitar textures, and the kind of low-end that earns its space instead of crowding it.

These beats are built for singers and songwriters who care about tone as much as they care about melody — artists who want their record to feel like something you'd play at the end of the night, not something that gets skipped after one listen. The palette leans both modern and classic, so you'll find beats that work for alternative R&B records, neo-soul throwbacks, and everything in between. If you're an indie artist shopping for a lead single, a label A&R sourcing for a project, or a songwriter building demos, this is the room for you.
