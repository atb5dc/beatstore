About the Catalog

Why these Latin beats?

The Latin catalog is built around the two sounds driving the urbano wave: reggaeton and Latin trap. The reggaeton side leans on the dembow rhythm that's been the pulse of the genre for twenty years — tight kicks, snappy snares, and the 90–100 BPM pocket that makes records move from the car to the club without losing energy. The Latin trap side sits heavier, with harder 808s, spacious arrangements, and the brooding atmosphere that built careers for artists like Bad Bunny, Anuel, and the new wave out of Puerto Rico and Medellín.

What sets this catalog apart is the instrumentation — live horns, stabs of brass, classic Latin percussion like timbales and congas woven into the modern grid, and guitar lines that give the beats a crossover warmth you won't get from template packs. Whether you're writing in Spanish, English, or switching between both, these beats are built to live on Latin DSPs, US urban playlists, and global dancefloors all at once.

Latin FAQ

Common Questions

Do you produce in specific Latin subgenres?

The catalog is strongest in reggaeton and Latin trap, which cover the majority of what's charting on urbano playlists right now. You'll also find dembow-driven records and crossover beats that blend Latin rhythms with hip hop and R&B elements. For more specialized subgenres — regional Mexican, bachata, merengue — reach out and I can point you toward what's in the vault or produce something custom.

Can I use these beats for Spanish-language songs distributed in Latin America?

Yes. Every license covers global distribution, including Latin American DSPs, radio, and venues.

Do you offer custom production with Latin vocalists?

Custom work is available — reach out with your vocalist info, tempo, and subgenre reference.
